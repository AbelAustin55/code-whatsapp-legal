You can ask us to review information associated with your chatbot interactions. This page explains what to send, how requests are handled and important limits.

You do not need to send passwords, OTPs, or confidential documents to request review or deletion of chatbot-held information.

What this page covers

This page explains how an individual can request review, correction or deletion of personal information processed for the CoDE WhatsApp Digital Assistant.

The request process concerns information controlled through the chatbot service. It does not automatically delete your WhatsApp account, phone-side chat history, Moodle account, examination records or other official University records held in separate systems.

How to submit a deletion request

  1. Send a request
    Email e-learning@mycodedigicampus.com with the subject CoDE WhatsApp Data Deletion Request.
  2. Identify the chatbot interaction
    State the WhatsApp number you used (including country code) and briefly describe whether you are requesting deletion, access or correction. Do not send passwords, PINs or OTP codes.
  3. Complete any necessary identity check
    CoDE may need to confirm that the request relates to your own information before taking action. Only a proportionate verification method should be used.
  4. Receive a response
    An authorised officer will review the request, explain what can be deleted or corrected, and communicate the outcome or any lawful retention reason.

Information that may be considered

Depending on what has been enabled in the service, a request may cover chatbot-held message identifiers, technical interaction records, support enquiries or related contact data under CoDE's control.

The current pilot stores processed message IDs in a local database to prevent duplicate automated replies. It does not store full WhatsApp messages or telephone numbers in that database. Any operational backups, server logs or future integrations must be assessed under the relevant retention arrangements.

Information that may not be removed immediately

Some data may need to be kept where required for legal compliance, records management, security investigations or the exercise of institutional obligations. A deletion request submitted here does not change official academic records maintained separately by the University.

Messages and information handled by WhatsApp/Meta are governed by Meta's own services and account controls. You can separately delete your conversation on your device, but doing so does not itself create a deletion request to CoDE or Meta.

What to expect after submitting

CoDE should acknowledge and assess valid requests using its approved data-protection and records-management process and respond within applicable legal timeframes. Requests may require clarification if the data cannot be located or the requester's identity cannot be confirmed.

Please avoid sending unnecessary documents. If sensitive verification is needed, ask for an authorised secure channel before submitting it.

Contact and other rights

For related questions about access, correction, objections or complaints, use the same contact address. If you remain concerned, you may also consult Ghana's Data Protection Commission regarding applicable rights and complaint procedures.

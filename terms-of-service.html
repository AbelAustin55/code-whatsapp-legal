Our chatbot is an additional way to find guidance and approved resources. It does not replace official University systems or academic decisions.

Use the assistant for information and support. Always verify high-stakes academic matters through the relevant official CoDE/UCC channel.

Purpose and acceptance

These Terms of Service apply to use of the CoDE WhatsApp Digital Assistant, a student-support channel associated with the College of Distance Education, University of Cape Coast. By communicating with the chatbot, you agree to use it responsibly and in line with these terms and applicable University rules.

The chatbot is designed to help users navigate approved educational resources, information notices and support pathways. Features may be introduced, changed or withdrawn during development and subsequent operation.

Services available

Depending on the stage of deployment, the assistant may provide interactive links or guidance about course materials, quizzes and examinations, academic calendars, Moodle assistance, announcements and tutor information.

Availability of individual services is not guaranteed. Some links may require the user to sign in on a separate official University platform. The current pilot uses demonstration content and does not access individual student academic records.

Appropriate use

When using the assistant, you agree to:

  • Provide accurate information where it is reasonably required for an academic-support request.
  • Use the service for lawful educational and support purposes.
  • Respect the privacy and rights of students, tutors and staff.
  • Avoid sending spam, abusive or threatening messages, malware, or content intended to disrupt the service.
  • Never share passwords, one-time codes or other authentication secrets through the chatbot.

CoDE may restrict access to the service where necessary to protect students, staff, systems and legitimate institutional operations, subject to applicable policies.

Official information and academic decisions

The assistant is a convenience and guidance service. It does not replace approved academic regulations, official examination instructions, course handbooks or decisions of authorised University officials.

Students remain responsible for confirming registration, fees, assessment schedules, examination eligibility, results and deadlines through the appropriate official CoDE/UCC channel. Where chatbot information conflicts with an official University notice, the authorised official source takes precedence.

Learning materials and external links

Course documents and resources linked by the assistant remain subject to applicable copyright, licence conditions and institutional access rules. A link does not grant permission to redistribute, modify or sell protected materials.

External or third-party pages, including WhatsApp and linked learning platforms, may have separate terms and privacy notices. CoDE is not responsible for changes made independently by those services.

Availability and limitations

The service may be temporarily unavailable during maintenance, network outages, WhatsApp platform interruptions or scheduled improvements. Information may occasionally be delayed, incomplete or inaccurate; important matters should be confirmed with authorised staff or official systems.

Any future AI-enabled responses must be identified and checked against approved institutional information. The chatbot is not an emergency, safeguarding or confidential counselling service.

Privacy and data handling

Information exchanged with the assistant is handled according to the Privacy Policy. If you need to request deletion of eligible chatbot data, follow the Data Deletion Instructions.

Updates and assistance

These terms may be revised to reflect new features, approved University requirements or changes in applicable law. The effective date shown above identifies the current published version.

We believe students should know what information is used to provide digital academic support, why it is needed and how to ask questions about it.

This notice applies to the CoDE WhatsApp Digital Assistant, including its limited development pilot and any approved features activated for student support.

About this notice

This Privacy Policy explains how the CoDE WhatsApp Digital Assistant handles personal information when someone contacts the service through WhatsApp or uses its related support pages. The assistant is a digital support initiative for the College of Distance Education (CoDE), University of Cape Coast, Ghana.

It covers chatbot interactions, technical processing needed to deliver messages, and approved links to learning or academic services. Separate University, Moodle, academic and WhatsApp policies may also apply when you use those services.

Information we may process

Depending on the features in use and the information you choose to provide, this may include:

  • WhatsApp contact details such as your telephone number and the profile name made available by WhatsApp.
  • Conversation information including your messages, menu choices, message identifiers, and the date or time of interactions.
  • Support requests such as the programme, level or course you select, and information voluntarily included in an enquiry.
  • Technical and security information such as webhook processing records, delivery statuses and limited server access logs needed to operate and protect the service.
  • Verified academic identifiers only where a later feature expressly requires authentication and is approved for access to student-specific records.

The initial development chatbot uses a limited test-recipient list and stores processed message identifiers locally to avoid duplicate replies. It is not yet connected to the student database or Moodle academic records.

Why we process information

Information is used for specified academic-support and service-operation purposes, which may include:

  • Responding to enquiries and navigating approved course materials, notices and support options.
  • Routing appropriate enquiries to authorised CoDE staff or official support channels.
  • Maintaining the reliability, security and integrity of chatbot messages.
  • Preventing repeated processing of the same incoming message.
  • Investigating technical faults, service misuse and legitimate support issues.

Features involving personal grades, registration details or other restricted student records must use appropriate identity verification and access permissions before they are enabled. We will provide additional notice if the purposes of processing change materially.

Services and information sharing

WhatsApp messages are carried through Meta's WhatsApp Business Platform. The chatbot may also rely on authorised hosting, networking and technical support providers. Where a user follows a link to an official learning platform, that platform's own privacy information may apply.

Access to information should be limited to approved staff and service providers who require it for their responsibilities. Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or a valid institutional process.

Providers may process information on infrastructure outside Ghana. Any such arrangements should be reviewed under applicable data protection requirements before general student deployment.

Storage, retention and security

The development service currently records processed message IDs in a local database for duplicate-message protection; it does not store full message text or telephone numbers in that database. Message contents still pass through WhatsApp and the chatbot's message-processing service. Standard server and hosting logs may record technical request details.

Records should be kept only as long as necessary for the stated purpose and in accordance with approved CoDE/UCC retention arrangements and applicable legal obligations. Operational retention periods and deletion procedures must be approved before expanding the service beyond its limited pilot.

Safeguards include signed webhook verification, restricted access to configuration secrets and test recipients, and controls for access to future student-specific services. No online system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices and rights

Under applicable law, including Ghana's Data Protection Act, 2012 (Act 843), individuals may have rights to request access to and correction of their personal data and to raise concerns about its handling, subject to applicable legal conditions.

You may stop contacting the chatbot at any time. You can also request review, correction or deletion of eligible chatbot-held information through the process described on the Data Deletion page. Deleting a WhatsApp chat on your phone does not automatically remove records held by the chatbot, Meta or official academic systems.

Safe use of the chatbot

Please do not send passwords, one-time verification codes, bank-card details, sensitive medical information or other confidential documents in an ordinary WhatsApp message. If a request involves personal academic records, use only the official identity-verification process provided by CoDE.

Responses and academic links are intended to support students; students should confirm important dates, assessment decisions and results through the relevant official University channel.

Changes and contact details

This notice may be updated when chatbot features, third-party services or data-handling arrangements change. The date at the top of this page will be revised when an update is published.

For privacy questions or requests concerning this chatbot, contact the CoDE eLearning support team using the address below. Where necessary, the request will be directed to the University's authorised data-protection or records office.
